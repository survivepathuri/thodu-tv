# Sessions with real users: template

This is a template for short, consented sessions where a person who might benefit from Thodu tries it on a TV. It is for learning where the app confuses people. It is **not** a clinical study and it does not test anyone's ability.

The real notes are kept **outside this repository**, in a private folder. Nothing from a session (no names, photos, recordings or personal details) is committed here.

## Before the session

- Choose a time and place the person is comfortable with. A family member the person trusts may sit beside them.
- Install the app (see [INSTALL_ON_TV.md](INSTALL_ON_TV.md)) and leave it in **Demo mode**: do not subscribe a real family phone and do not use a real notification name during a session, so nobody receives a real message by accident.
- The observer writes on paper or in a private file, not in the repository.

## Consent statement

Say this aloud (or give it written, in the person's language), and ask for a clear yes or no:

> I am testing a TV app to find out where it is hard to use. We are testing the app, not you. You can stop at any time, skip anything, and ask me to leave. I will not take photos or record your voice or screen. I will not write down your name or personal details. I may write a short note about what happened and, if you agree, one sentence you said in my own words. Do you agree to try it?

Record only **that consent was given** (yes or no, spoken or written, date). If the answer is no, thank the person and stop.

## What we will and will not record

| We will write down | We will not record |
|---|---|
| Which tasks were tried and whether they worked | Photos or video |
| Roughly how long each took | Voice recordings |
| What looked confusing (in the observer's words) | The person's name, address, health details or phone numbers |
| One paraphrased comment, only if the person agrees | Anything that could identify the person or the household |

Use a label such as "Participant 1". Add a general age band only if the person agrees.

## Tasks

Read each task in plain words and let the person try. Do not hint unless they ask; if you help, mark "with help".

1. Turn on scanning (single-button mode) and use it to move to a tile.
2. Open Help (in Demo mode, this sends only a demo request).
3. Send a test message in Settings, Family notifications (in Demo mode this is clearly marked as a demo).
4. Change the language.

Also note: the needs chosen on the setup screen, whether read-aloud or large text was on, and anything that failed because of the app (not because of the person).

## After the session

- Thank the person. Offer to reset the app ("Reset Thodu" in Settings) or uninstall it.
- Delete anything you wrote that could identify them.
- Turn network debugging off on the TV.

## How results will be reported

- Honestly and small: "n = 1", or however many people took part, in one household. These are anecdotes, not statistics and not a clinical study.
- Counts and plain descriptions (for example "needed help on the language step"), never percentages from tiny numbers.
- Problems are reported as they are, including failures. Quotes are paraphrased and used only with permission.
- Telugu and Hindi wording is still awaiting native-speaker review; if a person finds the wording wrong, that is a result to report, not a failure of the person.
